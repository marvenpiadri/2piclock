HELP & COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about x-facture and our free financial calculators.

help_outline Is x-facture completely free to use?

Yes! All invoicing tools, commercial document generators, retail POS register sessions, and standalone financial calculators are 100% free with no monthly subscription or hidden fees.

help_outline How do the standalone financial calculators work?

Each calculator operates as an isolated, mathematically validated tool with real-time reactive calculations, interactive Chart.js graphs, formula breakdowns, and preset parameters for Stripe, PayPal, VAT rates, and startup burn rates.

help_outline Can I print thermal receipts and PDF invoices?

Yes! You can instantly print or save standard A4/Letter PDF invoices with professional styling, as well as 80mm/58mm thermal receipts from the POS register.

help_outline Where is my data stored?

All data resides securely in your browser (LocalStorage and IndexedDB). You can export and backup your entire database anytime via JSON in the Settings tab.
